Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Townro Laundry ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate as a cloud-based laundry pickup and delivery service in Kothagudem, Telangana. Protecting your privacy is a top priority.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our WhatsApp booking bot or website, we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Doorstep pickup and delivery address / location pin
  • Order details (types of garments and service preferences)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to coordinate pickups, process laundry orders, send automated WhatsApp tracking updates via AiSensy, and provide customer support.

4. Data Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely via encrypted databases (Google Sheets / Make.com infrastructure).

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, reach out to us via WhatsApp or at support@townro.in.
