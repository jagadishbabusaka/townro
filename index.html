Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and doorstep pickup in Kothagudem & Palvancha.

How do I check rates and book a pickup?

Since pricing varies by city based on local partner operations, simply click any "Check Rates via WhatsApp" button. Our automated assistant will share the exact rate card for your city and help you schedule a pickup slot.

What is the minimum order for free doorstep pickup?

Free doorstep pickup and delivery is applicable for all standard orders across Kothagudem, Palvancha, Writer Colony, Rudrampur, and connecting areas.

What is the turnaround delivery time?

Our standard turnaround time is 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day delivery slots are also available upon special request via WhatsApp.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) via QR code upon delivery, as well as Cash on Delivery (COD).
